# Privacy Policy — Affiliate Bot

This app is a personal automation tool used by its owner to
create pins on their own Pinterest boards.

## Data we access

Through the Pinterest API, the app accesses only the owner's own
account data: board list (to know where to save pins) and pin
creation on the owner's boards.

Scopes used: boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write.

## Data we store

- Pinterest OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and never shared.
- A record of posted product IDs is kept to avoid duplicate pins.

## Data sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties. Personal use only.
