Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This app ("Smart Shoppers Hub Bot") is a personal automation tool used by its owner to create pins on their own Pinterest boards.

Data we access

Through the Pinterest API (v5), the app accesses only the owner's own Pinterest account data needed for its function:

  • Board list and board IDs (to know where to save pins)
  • Creating pins (title, description, image URL, link) on the owner's boards

OAuth scopes used: boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write.

Data we store

  • Pinterest OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and never shared.
  • A record of already-posted product IDs is kept to avoid duplicate pins.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties. The app is for single-owner, personal use only.

Affiliate disclosure

Pins created by this app may contain affiliate links. Descriptions include an #ad disclosure as required.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the app owner through the GitHub repository.
