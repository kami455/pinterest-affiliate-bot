About us

Smart Shopper's Hub is a personal online shopping recommendations brand. We discover the best daily deals, unique gadgets, and trendy home finds from across the globe and share them with our audience on Pinterest.

Our Pinterest app

Smart Shoppers Hub Bot is our own automation tool. It runs on a schedule and creates pins on our own Pinterest boards to share AliExpress affiliate products we promote. Every pin includes a clear affiliate disclosure (#ad). The app is for single-owner, personal business use only — it never accesses other users' accounts or data.

The app uses the Pinterest API with the following scopes: boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write.
